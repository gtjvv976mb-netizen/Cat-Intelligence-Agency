CoinMarketCat, the agentic trading cat.

Agentic trading for Solana, in plain English. Write a strategy, pick up to ten tokens and set hard limits. On your schedule CoinMarketCat asks a model what to do; then code, not the model, decides what is allowed. It starts on paper, spends nothing until you arm it live, and you can pause it, sell everything or withdraw at any time.

How it works

Your plan in plain English. Its limits in code.

An agent you describe, run from your own browser. The model proposes; deterministic code disposes.

  1. 1
    You

    Write the strategy

    In your own words, in any language, up to 4,000 characters: what to buy, when to sell, what to avoid. It is the strategy the model follows, and never a limit — nothing written there can widen one.

  2. 2
    You

    Pick the tokens and the limits

    Up to ten tokens: the Solana majors preset — JitoSOL, JUP, JTO, PYTH, RAY, BONK, WIF and cbBTC, each read on chain before it was listed — or tokens you add, each read on chain when you save it. It is settled in USDC by default, or USDT: every buy spends it, every sell returns it. SOL itself is not tradable in this version.

  3. 15′
    Every 15, 30 or 60 minutes

    It asks a model

    With your own Anthropic API key, the agent shows a model each token's price, 1-hour and 24-hour change, volume, liquidity, and RSI, moving averages and returns computed from 15-minute candles. A figure a source did not give is sent as missing, never guessed. The model answers buy, sell or hold, with a reason.

  4. 30″
    Every half minute

    Code applies the limits

    Every proposal is clamped or refused by name against your limits. The stop loss, the take profit and the daily drawdown breaker check every half minute on their own, whether or not the model answers. The model cannot change a limit, trade outside your list, or withdraw.

  5. ✎
    Always

    It writes everything down

    Every decision with its rationale, every refusal with its reason, every fill with its transaction signature, the profit and loss, the win rate, the worst drawdown, and the tokens each model call used.

If the model cannot be reached, answers badly, or your key is refused, that tick opens nothing new — and the protections run anyway. Nothing about this agent's returns has been measured: no win rate, no return, no drawdown from a real run.

Your limits

You set the numbers. The model cannot move them.

The defaults below are the agent's starting values, not recommendations. Change any of them in Options; a live agent must be re-armed when you do.

Per token
$25default

$25 per token at most. A bigger buy is clamped to what is left under it.

In tokens
60%default

At most 60% of the vault in tokens; the rest stays in the settlement token.

Stop and take
8% · 15%default

An 8% stop loss and a 15% take profit, each against the position's entry price, each selling it in full.

Daily drawdown
5%default

A 5% daily drawdown from the vault's value at the start of the UTC day trips a breaker: no new buys, or sell everything — your choice. It resets at UTC midnight.

Trades a day
6default

6 trades a day by the model. A stop loss or take profit never counts and is never refused.

Slippage
100bps default

100 bps, written into every Jupiter instruction and checked before anything is signed.

Minimums
$10 · $50fixed

A $10 minimum trade and a $50 minimum vault. These two are not dials.

Schedule
30min default

The model is asked every 15, 30 or 60 minutes. Each call is billed to your key.

Paper and live

Paper first. Live from a wallet you fund.

Paper is the default. Live trades from the autopilot wallet, a separate wallet inside the extension that signs without a window each time.

Paper

Fills at Jupiter's quotes

A paper vault — $100 unless you change it — and every buy and sell filled at the price Jupiter quotes for it. Nothing is signed and nothing moves.

  • No wallet and no RPC needed: only your API key.
  • The same limits, the same protections, the same journal.
  • A quote is not a fill: a real trade can come in lower, down to the slippage cap.
Live

The autopilot wallet signs

Fund it from Phantom with at least $50 of USDC or USDT, and a little SOL for network fees. Unlock it, and type the sentence the agent prints, naming the wallet, every limit and every token.

  • Checked before signing: only your settlement token and your listed tokens, either way; Jupiter's transaction decoded; the trade simulated on your RPC.
  • Encrypted under your passphrase (at least 12 characters, never stored) with AES-GCM and a PBKDF2 key, in your browser only.
  • Unlocked per session — eight hours by default, gone when the browser closes. A locked wallet signs nothing, sells included.
  • It signs every buy and sell without asking you.
Your controls

Pause: the model is not asked; the stop loss, take profit and breaker keep running. Liquidate all: every position sold back to the settlement token through the same checks. Withdraw: the autopilot wallet swept back to your Phantom address. Only you can withdraw — it is not something the model can ask for, and there is no path from its answer to a withdrawal.

What it costs

A private key in a browser is a bigger target than Phantom: while it is unlocked, malware on your machine or a hostile extension could read it. Every model call is billed to your own API key. And it is new: no Autopilot trade, fund or sweep has yet been made on mainnet. Fund it only with what you can lose.

The full threat model is in docs/session-wallet.md. Only one file in the repository may touch that key; a test scans every other source file on every run.

What runs where

On your computer, while Chrome is open.

It runs while Chrome is open on this computer, not in a cloud around the clock. Close Chrome or let the computer sleep, and nothing runs: not the model, not the stop loss. It is spot only, with no leverage.

In your browser

The agent, its limits, its journal and the autopilot wallet, in the extension you built. Your API key is kept there too, and sent only to Anthropic's API.

Anthropic's API

Each model call, with your own API key, billed to your own account. You pick the model from the list your key returns; by default, the newest it lists.

Public market data

Prices, volume and liquidity from DexScreener, candles from GeckoTerminal, quotes and transactions from Jupiter — all keyless, all rate-limited on your side.

Nowhere else

There is no agency server and no account. Live trades are read and sent through the RPC you choose. Sharing or selling a strategy to other people is not built.

The other lane

Snipurr, the sniper lane.

Snipurr, the sniper cat, ships in the same extension. It crouches ten seconds over every new pump.fun launch and pounces only if the launch is still at or above its entry price. It takes profit at 1.5× by default, sells a position still under its entry price at ninety seconds, and leaves any position after 180. It also sells at your stop — 20% of entry by default, wide because at the smallest ticket the network fees make a tighter one unfundable — and when the launch's creator starts selling. A setup page opens when you install the extension. Saving puts the cat in Observe.

Observe first Crouch 10 s Take 1.5× Out by 180 s
Per trade
0.005SOL default

The most it spends on one launch, never above 1 SOL. Above this canary size it makes you choose a stop.

Daily budget
0.01SOL default

Every buy is charged to a rolling day. At the cap it stops buying; exits still run.

Stock focus
noneSOL only

List up to eight tokenised stocks to pay with, in each stock's own units; the setup page offers GLDx, TSLAx, SPYx, AAPLx and NVDAx. With none listed, it refuses them all.

Pool venue
offdefault

New stock-paired pools, traded through Jupiter. Off until you switch it on.

Approving in Phantom is the default for Snipurr: every buy and sell is one Phantom window on the console tab. A buy window left open past 25 seconds is abandoned; a declined sell is asked again 8 seconds later, and an unanswered one is dropped after 32 seconds and asked again. On Autopilot the autopilot wallet signs instead. Arming is a sentence you type:

I arm HAWK-AI v1 for <your wallet>: 0.005 SOL per launch, 0.01 SOL per day, sold in full at the take, the stop, the creator's exit or the clock

The record Snipurr learned from

Its rule is what the Claude Company desk's HAWK-AI sniper lane taught by losing. Its live trades, read back off Solana mainnet on 2026-09-17:

10 up · 48 downits first 58 round trips, −1.58 SOL net
0 of 9entries under three seconds after launch that won
0 of 18positions left open to its old ten-minute clock that won
28 of 64coins that reached 1.5× after the fill; the lane took it on 5

Entries ten seconds and later did better — 4 of 10 won — but ten trades is not a sample. None of this is evidence of an edge. Nothing has been measured yet about stock-paired launches or pools: no win rate, no follow-through.

Install

Build it yourself. It is not in a store.

You build it from the source, read what you build if you like, and load it into Chrome 116 or later. The build needs Node.js 22.13 to 24.

  1. Clone the repository. 
    git clone https://github.com/gtjvv976mb-netizen/Cat-Intelligence-Agency
cd Cat-Intelligence-Agency
  2. Install and build. The extension is written to dist/. 
    npm ci
npm run build
  3. Load it unpacked. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, press Load unpacked and choose the dist folder.
  4. Set up the agent. In Options → Agent: name it, write the strategy, tick the tokens, set the limits, paste your own Anthropic API key and pick a model (or keep the newest). Start it on paper from the popup.
  5. Watch it on paper. Read its journal: every decision and its reason, every refusal, every paper fill. Go live only after that — fund the autopilot wallet from Phantom, unlock it, and type the sentence it prints.
  6. For Snipurr and for funding, paste an RPC URL from a provider such as Helius, Triton or QuickNode (the public Solana RPC refuses browsers), open the console page and connect Phantom there.
Risk notice

Read this before you fund anything.

  • This cat's own live record is unmeasured. Nobody has a track record for the agent, on paper or live, for Snipurr, or for Autopilot. There is no return to quote, and none is quoted.
  • A model's judgement is not an edge. It decides from a snapshot of prices and a few indicators, and nothing shows that it decides well. The limits bound what it can lose in a day; they do not make it right.
  • It only runs while Chrome is open. Asleep, closed or offline, nothing runs — not the model, not the stop loss.
  • Launch sniping loses money more often than not. The lane this cat learned from has been unprofitable. The desk's HAWK-AI lane went 10 up and 48 down over its first 58 round trips, for −1.58 SOL, and every modelled variant of it still lost. Under the fee model the desk uses, a Snipurr round trip at the 0.005 SOL canary has to rise about 22% just to break even.
  • Costs add up. Every model call is billed to your API key, every trade pays a network fee in SOL, and a round trip pays the pool's fees and slippage twice.
  • Tokens carry their issuers' controls. USDC, USDT and cbBTC can be frozen by their issuers; the xStock mints Snipurr can pay with can be frozen or paused.
  • Software fails. A feed can lag, a quote can move, an RPC can drop a transaction — then you act by hand.

Run the agent on paper and Snipurr in Observe first. Only fund what you can afford to lose. This is a user-operated tool, not financial advice and not a signal service.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How much does it make?

Unmeasured. The agent has no track record, paper or live, and we do not quote returns or win rates. Its journal is how you find out for yourself, on paper, before you fund anything.

Does it trade around the clock?

No. It runs while Chrome is open on your computer. The model is asked on your schedule and the protections check every half minute, but only while the browser is running.

What does it cost?

There is no subscription and no agency fee. You pay for your own model calls through your own Anthropic API key, the network's fees on every live trade, the pools' fees, and whatever your RPC provider charges.

Can the AI withdraw my money, or change my limits?

No. It can propose buy, sell or hold for the tokens you listed, and nothing else. The limits are code it cannot change, every live trade is checked before signing, and only you can withdraw — from the popup, to your Phantom address.

What if the model is down, or answers nonsense?

That tick opens nothing new, the failure is written in the journal, and the stop loss, take profit and daily drawdown breaker keep running as usual.

Which model does it use?

Whichever your key may use and you pick in Options; by default, the newest one the API lists for your key.

Does it use leverage?

No. Spot only: it buys a token with the settlement token and sells it back. No leverage, no shorting, no borrowing.

Does it ever see my Phantom key?

No. Live, it trades from the autopilot wallet, a separate wallet it created, encrypted under your passphrase, in your browser only. Phantom only funds it, with one approval, and receives the withdrawal.

Why does Snipurr wait ten seconds? Isn't the point of sniping to be first?

The desk tried being first. Its entries under three seconds after launch won 0 of 9. A launch you can reach that fast has often already been bought in bulk by its insiders, and arriving right behind them makes you the one they sell to. It is a rule learned from a small losing sample, not proof of anything.

Which browsers does it run in?

Chrome 116 or later, loaded unpacked, with the Phantom extension installed for funding. It is built as a Manifest V3 extension; other Chromium browsers may work but are untested.

Who makes it? Is it CoinMarketCap?

It is Agent 001 of Cat Intelligence Agency, an independent software project — not a government agency. CoinMarketCat is not affiliated with CoinMarketCap, and not affiliated with Anthropic, Phantom, pump.fun, Jupiter or the xStocks issuer either; it works with their public software and programs.
