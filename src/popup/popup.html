        Export the key (recovery)

        This shows the autopilot wallet's private key once, in the form Phantom and Solflare import. Anyone who sees it can spend everything in the wallet. Use it to recover funds, then treat the wallet as exposed: sweep it and replace it.

        Replace the autopilot wallet

        For a wallet you exported or no longer trust. Sweep it back first: a wallet that still holds SOL above the rent floor, any token, or a live position is not replaced. The old key is discarded; the new wallet starts locked and empty.

        A key in a browser is a bigger attack surface than Phantom: while the autopilot wallet is unlocked, malware on this machine or a hostile extension could read it. Your exposure is what you fund it with. Lock clears the unlocked key. Sweep back when you are done.
